Try PLGen in Google Docs

A sidebar for Google Docs that checks a document's writing pattern and issues a real Provenance Label without leaving the page.

Private pilot link This isn't published to the Google Workspace Marketplace yet — you're getting early access directly. Please don't share this link further without checking with us first.

Get set up

Make your own copy

Click below to make your own copy of the PLGen-connected Google Doc. Nothing you write is shared back with us — the copy is fully yours.

Get Your Copy →

Open the copy, then open PLGen

In your new copy, find Provenance in the menu bar and open it — the sidebar will appear on the right.

Authorize it — one expected warning

The first time, Google will show a screen saying it hasn't verified this app. That's expected — we're in private pilot, not published yet.

What to click Click Advanced, then click Go to [project name] (unsafe). This just means Google hasn't reviewed the app yet — it doesn't mean anything is wrong.

Use it

Check This Document reads the doc's own revision history and reports on its writing pattern. Issue Label creates a real Provenance Label from your self-reported split. Apply Label inserts both into the document — re-applying replaces what was there before, so it never duplicates.

Tell us how it goes

This is early and we want your honest reaction — what's confusing, what's missing, whether the label actually says what you'd want a reader to see. Reply directly to whoever sent you this link.
